The problem

A gym does not lose members. It forgets them.

Nothing here is a software problem. Every one of them is a memory problem — and that is exactly what software is for.

Renewals leak

The register lies

Enquiries vanish

The owner guesses

How much came in this month? Who owes money today? The answer lives in a notebook, or in his head.

The gym owner is not disorganised. He is running a floor, coaching members and taking cash at the desk — all at once. He needs the remembering done for him.
