# UAT Checklist (sign-off with owner)

Run on staging with seeded data, then repeat critical items on production before go-live. Tick, date and initial each line.

## A. Website (on the owner's phone and one laptop)
- [ ] Landing opens fast on 4G; video plays; pause works
- [ ] All photos are real and approved; no DEMO labels in production
- [ ] Prices correct for men and women, all plans
- [ ] Owner story facts correct; photos approved
- [ ] Hours, address, phone, map, WhatsApp link correct
- [ ] Lead form → owner receives WhatsApp alert
- [ ] Legal pages reviewed (Privacy, Terms, Refund, Contact)
- [ ] Hindi toggle reads naturally

## B. Sign-up & payment
- [ ] Selfie works on Android Chrome, iPhone Safari
- [ ] Camera denied → upload fallback works
- [ ] Under-18 DOB shows guardian message
- [ ] Payment ₹1 live test (or smallest plan with refund) succeeds; receipt on WhatsApp
- [ ] Pay at reception → appears in CRM → desk payment activates

## C. QR
- [ ] Printed poster scans from 1 m in reception light
- [ ] Existing customer flow asks "What is your month-end date?" and cannot skip
- [ ] Verification approve/edit/reject work

## D. CRM — owner 5-task test (unaided, < 2 min each)
- [ ] 1. Find a member and tell their fee date
- [ ] 2. Renew a member with cash
- [ ] 3. Call first person on call list and record outcome
- [ ] 4. Approve a QR submission
- [ ] 5. See who came today
Plus:
- [ ] Voice button speaks Hindi
- [ ] Birthday wish sends
- [ ] Mark left + undo
- [ ] Reception staff login cannot see money totals/settings
- [ ] Settings: change a price → landing updates within 5 min

## E. WhatsApp
- [ ] All templates approved (EN + HI)
- [ ] Test member on allowlist receives PRE_7 → renew link works
- [ ] Unsubscribe button → confirmation → member Left in CRM → no further messages next slot
- [ ] Restart button works within 7 days
- [ ] Owner digest arrives 08:30 with correct numbers
- [ ] Kill switch stops all automatic messages

## F. Attendance kiosk
- [ ] Survives power cut and reboots into kiosk mode
- [ ] Shadow-mode accuracy report meets targets
- [ ] Expired member → discreet screen + owner alert
- [ ] Unknown → keypad fallback works
- [ ] Offline 1 h → events sync after reconnect
- [ ] Face consent withdrawn → not recognised after sync
- [ ] Signage installed

## G. Operations
- [ ] Backup restore drill passed (date: ____)
- [ ] Uptime monitoring alerts reach vendor phone
- [ ] Owner has training video + laminated guide
- [ ] Support contact and hours agreed
