# Owner Story — Interview Guide

Goal: a short, verified, human story for the Champion section, social posts and the About page. Record audio (with permission), 30–40 minutes, in Hindi or whichever language the owner prefers. Bring a printout of these questions; don't read them like a form — let him talk.

## Facts to verify (bring documents)
1. Full name, and how members address you (e.g., "Sir", "Bhaiya", "Coach", first name + ji).
2. Discipline/sport (bodybuilding, powerlifting, weightlifting, wrestling, other?).
3. Exact championship name, organising federation, city, year(s), weight category, position/medal.
4. Other titles (state, district, university, international).
5. When did you start training? Where? Who was your coach?
6. Why did you open Max Fitness Gym in 2000? Where was it first? What did it look like?
7. Certificates, medals, newspaper cuttings, old competition photos — may we photograph them?

## Story questions
8. What's the hardest thing you did to win the national title?
9. What did being a champion teach you that you now teach members?
10. Tell us about a member who started as a complete beginner and changed a lot (with their permission).
11. What do you tell someone who's nervous to join a gym?
12. How do you make sure women feel comfortable and respected at your gym?
13. What should a member never do in the gym? What should they always do?
14. In one line, why should someone in Indirapuram train with you?
15. Your favourite exercise and your favourite cheat meal? (for social content)

## Deliverables after interview
- `interview-notes.md` (summary + key quotes, transcribed)
- `approved-story.md` (≤ 180 words EN + HI, owner-approved, facts verified against documents)
- `timeline.md` (verified year-by-year entries)
- `quotes.md` (3–5 short quotes the owner approves)
- Photos of documents (flat, even light, ≥ 3000 px)
- Signed permission to publish
